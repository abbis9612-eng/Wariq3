## INSTRUCTIONS FOR GROK (apply to every image)

Realistic game art for a modern military grand-strategy game on a map.

- Photorealistic, like a real photo or high-resolution satellite image. Not cartoon, not toy, not painting, not 3D-render look.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags, no watermark. No people's bodies, no blood.
- **Top-down sprites (PART 1, PART 2):** camera STRICTLY above, looking straight down, orthographic, no tilt. The nose / bow points to the **RIGHT**. Object centered, fills ~85% of the width, nothing cut off. **Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF)**, no ground, no water, no shadow.
- **Research cards (PART 3):** full-bleed photo, wide 1536×1024, cinematic, realistic.
- **National designs (PART 4):** ONE image with TWO views of the SAME vehicle on a pure white background: **LEFT half = strictly top-down view, nose to the right; RIGHT half = side view, nose to the right.** Both the same scale. The whole vehicle is painted a **plain uniform light grey (#B8B8B8), matte, no camouflage, no markings, no flags, no numbers**. 2048×1024.
