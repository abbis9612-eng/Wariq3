## INSTRUCTIONS FOR GROK (apply to every image)

These are top-down game sprites for a realistic naval warfare game, seen from a satellite / drone directly above.

- **Camera: STRICTLY top-down (orthographic), exactly above the object, looking straight down.** We must NOT see the side of a hull or a vehicle, only the deck, roof and top surfaces. No perspective, no tilt.
- **Orientation: the bow / nose points to the RIGHT side of the image.** The object is horizontal and centered.
- Photorealistic, like a high-resolution satellite photo of the real ship: real deck markings, real paint, real details. Not a cartoon, not a toy.
- **Ships, missiles and aircraft: pure flat white background (#FFFFFF).** No water, no wake, no shadow, nothing else in the image.
- **Effects (smoke, fire, explosion, foam): pure flat black background (#000000)**, so they can be blended in the game.
- The object fills about 90% of the image width. Nothing cut off.
- Wide image 2048×1024 for ships (ships are long and thin); square 1024×1024 for effects.
- No text, no letters, no hull numbers, no logos, no watermark.
- Accurate to the real class: correct shape, correct number and position of turrets, launchers, radars and the island.
