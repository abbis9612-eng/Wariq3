## INSTRUCTIONS FOR GROK (read first, apply to every image)

You are making game art for a realistic modern military strategy game. All images must look like one consistent set.

**Style (every image):**
- Photorealistic 3D render, like a high-end military simulation game asset. Real materials: painted metal, rubber tyres, glass, matte camouflage paint with slight wear and dust.
- **Background: pure flat white (#FFFFFF), nothing else.** No ground texture, no scenery, no sky, no people, no other vehicles.
- **No cast shadow on the background** (I add shadows myself). Light only on the object itself.
- Lighting: soft daylight from the **top-left**, the same in every image.
- One object per image, centered, filling about 80% of the frame, fully visible (nothing cut off at the edges).
- Square image, 1024 × 1024.
- **No text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no flags painted larger than real life.**
- Accuracy matters more than drama: correct number of wheels or axles, correct number of missile canisters or tubes, correct shape and proportions of the real system. If unsure, choose the most common real configuration, and do not invent extra weapons.

**Two camera views (each item says which ones to make):**
- **View A, "card":** three-quarter view from the front-left, camera about 35° above the ground, as if photographing the vehicle for a catalogue.
- **View B, "map":** straight top-down view (camera directly above, looking down, no perspective tilt), with the front of the vehicle, ship or aircraft **pointing to the right**.

Make the images in the numbered order below. For each number, make View A, then View B.
