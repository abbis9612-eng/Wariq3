## INSTRUCTIONS FOR GROK (apply to every image)

Realistic game sprites for a modern military strategy game seen on a map.

- Photorealistic, like a real photo / satellite image. Not cartoon, not toy, not painting.
- **No people's bodies, no blood, no gore.** No text, no letters, no logos, no flags, no watermark.
- PART 1 and PART 3 (missiles, wreckage): **pure flat white background (#FFFFFF)**, object centered, fills ~85% of width, nothing cut off.
- PART 2 (explosions, smoke, flashes): **pure flat black background (#000000)** so they can be blended additively in the game.
- PART 4 and PART 5 (project and research cards): full-bleed photo, wide 1536×1024, no text.
